How the money moves

  1. 1

    Fees route to the bridge

    The coin's pump.fun fee-sharing config sends 100% of creator fees to the bridge wallet, and the coin is registered to an X handle.

  2. 2

    Converted to dollars

    Every ten minutes the bridge distributes accrued fees, swaps the SOL to USDC on Jupiter, and credits the handle.

  3. 3

    Claimed with X

    The handle's owner signs in with X and withdraws to a Solana wallet. Once a wallet is linked, balances over the auto-payout floor are sent automatically.

Route a coin's fees

Launched on three.ws?

Open the coin's Fees & rewards panel from your tokens dashboard or the agent page and choose Pay an X account in USDC. It writes the split and registers the handle in one step.

Any other pump.fun coin

  1. With the coin's creator wallet, set its fee-sharing config to one shareholder: the bridge wallet below, at 100%.
  2. Link that creator wallet to your three.ws account, then register the coin here.
loading bridge wallet

A pump.fun fee-sharing update is usually final. Check the handle before you sign: a registered coin cannot be pointed at a different handle later.
